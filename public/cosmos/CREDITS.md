## Other scenes

Solar navigation, galactic map, deep-field galaxies, schematic diagrams and cosmic light network are original procedural visualizations in this project. Starfield is generated, not a measured catalog. The black-hole renderer uses the project model described in the 0.6 and 0.7 documentation.

## Journey Cinematography Pass (2026-10-02)

The current main journey loads only the Blue Marble Earth surface texture from these image assets. Earth is a rotating 3D sphere with independently animated procedural clouds and atmosphere. Solar system, galaxy direction, star-forming region, binary system and pulsar/remnant are now real-time coded spatial scenes. Soft particles and luminous billboards remain deliberate 2.5D components.

The Webb files above are retained for historical archive/reference use and credit preservation. They are not requested or shown as the current main journey environments. Their treatment notes describe the previous reconstruction pass.

The tidal-disruption source is an original procedural thin-plane emission and authored motion demonstration integrated with the Schwarzschild ray tracer. It is not footage or a stellar fluid simulation.

## Visual Refinement & Cosmic Events Pass

No additional external images were introduced. The Blue Marble surface remains the only main-journey photographic texture. Clouds and atmosphere are coded approximations. Key nebulae now include bounded 3D procedural density integration; remote galaxies are original 2.5D procedural emission planes baked into the shared sky.

The stellar body and small debris are animated 3D emission/occlusion proxies intersected along the curved light path. Tidal streams and captured gas retain a thin-plane emission approximation. Matter motion and disk responses are authored, not fluid, matter-geodesic or energy-conserving simulations. Screenshots in refinement-stills are direct captures of this project's Film Mode, not external assets.
